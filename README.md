📊 Sales Forecast Analysis

📌 Overview

This project analyzes sales data to identify key trends, top-performing products, and seasonal sales patterns. The goal is to gain insights into sales growth and forecast future trends based on historical data.

📂 Dataset
	•	Source: Superstore Sales Dataset (Kaggle)
	•	Data Includes:
	•	Order details (Order ID, Order Date, Ship Date)
	•	Customer information (Customer ID, Segment, Region, Country, City)
	•	Product details (Product ID, Category, Sub-Category, Product Name)
	•	Sales performance (Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount)

🛠️ Analysis Steps
	1.	Data Cleaning & Preparation
	•	Converted dates into proper format.
	•	Extracted year, quarter, and month from order dates.
	•	Verified and cleaned column names.
	2.	Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
	•	Calculated total sales per year to analyze trends.
	•	Identified top 3 products by revenue for each year.
	•	Determined most successful quarters across multiple years.
	•	Analyzed monthly sales trends to observe seasonal variations.
	3.	Data Visualization
	•	Annual Sales Growth (Line Graph)
	•	Top 3 Products by Year (Bar Chart)
	•	Quarterly Sales Trends (Line Graph)
	•	Most Successful Quarters (Bar Chart)
	•	Monthly Sales Trends (Line Graph)

📊 Key Findings
	•	Sales trended upward over the years, with 2018 being the most successful year.
	•	Quarter 4 consistently had the highest sales volume, indicating strong holiday sales.
	•	The top-selling products varied across years, with some maintaining strong sales across multiple periods.
	•	Monthly sales trends revealed seasonal spikes, especially in Q4.
